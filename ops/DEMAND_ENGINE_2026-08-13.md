# Decay Labs Demand Engine

Date: 2026-08-13. Evidence boundary: the product, contract, lore and distribution surfaces were inspected; demand claims remain hypotheses until real people use them. No fake scarcity, engagement, collector or sale is assumed.

## A. Demand Engine

The product now has a three-stage demand path: **visual encounter → personal match → exact Subject record**. The new `/find-your-subject` experience turns the 1,000-item corpus into a choice rather than a catalogue. Ownership is introduced only after the user has recovered and understood a specific record.

First sources: 20 meaningful Farcaster replies/collaborations, 20 Base/Farcaster builders reached through shipped proof, 20 onchain-art artists/curators, 20 existing personal network testers, and 20 people entering through share links. The first objective is 100 relevant visits, not 10,000 followers.

## B. Cold Start Solution

Use one repeatable public ritual: **Which witness finds you?** Each participant receives a deterministic, shareable Subject. A weekly archive prompt asks people to compare interpretation rather than promote a marketplace link. Cold-start loop: visitor → three choices → named faction/Subject → share → follower opens the same record → takes a different path.

## C. First 100 People Strategy

Target mix: 25 onchain-art collectors, 20 artists, 15 curators/writers, 15 Base builders, 15 Farcaster/Base App users and 10 Mini App/open-source builders. Give each group a reason: collectors get identity and provenance; artists get interpretation; curators get a coherent corpus; builders get public implementation proof; users get a zero-wallet discovery experience.

## D. Collector Desire Engine

Desire should come from **attachment, not urgency**. A Subject becomes preferable through five factual/narrative layers: personal match, faction affiliation, exact memory fragment, callsign/condition, and verifiable onchain record. Never call these financial rarity. The matching result explains why that Subject was recovered, giving #554 or #846 a reason to matter beyond price.

Supply presentation options, without changing listings:

1. **Curated window:** show 12–24 visually distinct records; all 1,000 remain searchable.
2. **Faction shelves:** five narrative groups, avoiding a wall of 800 listings.
3. **Recovered signals:** feature only Subjects selected by real matches/shares that week.
4. **Archive availability:** show listing state on each exact record; do not claim artificial scarcity.

## E. Subject Differentiation System

The art remains unchanged. Differentiation comes from deterministic faction, callsign, condition, memory object, chapter and signal ID; faction color and cinematic crop are presentation aids. Use “rare” only after computing trait distribution from canonical metadata. Do not pretend generated narrative labels are onchain rarity.

## F. Product Loop

**OPEN → DISCOVER → CHOOSE → MATCH → LEARN → SHARE → RETURN.** Implemented pieces: archive/collection, three-choice match, exact Subject page, share endpoint, analytics and repeatable deep link. Next low-cost piece: a weekly editorial “Recovered Record,” selected manually from real response data rather than a cron-generated daily gimmick.

## G. Viral Loop

Match result → exact Subject share URL → Subject-specific embed → recipient opens record → recipient takes own match. Friction: square IPFS previews may crop, Farcaster graph is tiny, and a share is not measurable when a client withholds completion. Test 10 real shares before building 1,000 dynamic cards.

## H. Retention Loop

Return reasons: weekly recovered record, new lore checkpoint, community comparison, genuine collect event and shipped product change. No XP, streak or points. Measure 7-day returning sessions only after enough traffic exists.

## I. Community Without Discord

Use recurring Farcaster formats: two-Subject interpretation, faction dilemma, Archive checkpoint and real build result. Replies become the community surface; the site stores no social profile. Discord remains deferred until there are enough holders/contributors to sustain it.

## J. Content Compounding Engine

Inputs: 1,000 profiles × 5 factions × 100 chapters × real product/onchain events. A script may propose Subject, hook, visual and destination; a person approves the voice. Limit to 3–4 original casts weekly plus meaningful replies. Event-driven lore is allowed only for verifiable events: a confirmed collect, holder change, release or measured share milestone.

## K. Collaboration Engine

- Artist × Subject interpretation: one real Subject, artist’s reading, both audiences receive attribution.
- Writer × Archive entry: a short canonical-adjacent interpretation clearly labeled guest work.
- Builder × checkout/share experiment: reusable code plus live Decay proof.
- Curator × six-record window: curator explains selection; no paid shill script.

## L. Vercel Growth Opportunities

Highest ROI now: static global delivery, exact share endpoint cached at CDN, deterministic result URLs, privacy-safe funnel logs and fast rollback. Next: 3:2 branded cards for the top proven Subjects, not all 1,000. Avoid per-user server rendering: matching runs client-side and stores no personal answer data. Do not add Edge Config, databases or feature-flag services until traffic justifies them.

## M. GitHub / Open Source Leverage

`base-miniapp-buy-starter` is the strongest reusable asset. Package one audited release with architecture, live demo, threat boundaries and tests. Distribution loop: useful starter → builder use/star → Base/Farcaster discussion → builder credibility → Decay discovery. Do not create empty releases or fake usage.

## O. Project Moat

Current moat is weak-to-moderate: the visual base pose is copyable and social proof is absent. Defensible assets can compound from the 1,000-Subject corpus, consistent archive language, collector history, faction interpretations and the working discovery/share/onchain system. Lore depth becomes a moat only when people use and reference it.

## P. Three Radical Product Directions

1. **Interactive identity archive (recommended):** matching, comparing and interpreting Subjects; NFT ownership is the durable record layer.
2. **Collaborative recovered canon:** real community decisions alter selected lore checkpoints, with transparent version history.
3. **Public archive protocol:** reusable open-source tools let other creators build verifiable Subject-style records; Decay is the flagship corpus.

## Q. One Big Bet

**Make “Find Your Subject” the front door.** It attacks catalogue fatigue, base-pose repetition, cold start, sharing, differentiation and collector attachment with one coherent mechanism. It is live, deterministic, wallet-free, privacy-safe and directly connected to exact records.

## R. Would this matter without NFTs?

Before this pass: mostly no; it behaved like an attractive sales site. After the matching/interpretation loop: potentially yes. A person can now discover a character, understand a faction, share a result and return for archive changes without buying. The answer becomes convincingly “yes” only if real users repeat or share the experience.
