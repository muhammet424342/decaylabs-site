## Inventory release policy

Do not list hundreds of items at the same price. It makes supply look abandoned and removes discovery.

1. Keep a maximum of 24 intentionally selected Subjects visible at a time.
2. Release six per faction/story beat, not all at once.
3. Attach every promoted listing to a memory fragment or chapter.
4. Do not publish fake floor, rarity or “last chance” claims.
5. Review listings weekly. Replace stale inventory only when a new story beat supports it.
6. Do not undercut a genuine collector listing with founder inventory.
7. Record token, list price, date, story post and outcome in an inventory sheet.

Curated starting set: `1, 4, 7, 9, 13, 18, 21, 24, 31, 44, 57, 72, 88, 101, 144, 233, 377, 404, 512, 616, 707, 808, 909, 1000`.

Only tokens with confirmed active listings should be linked in sales posts. The website checkout API verifies live OpenSea orders instead of displaying invented availability.
