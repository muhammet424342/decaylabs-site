# 90-day organic growth execution plan

## Objective

Make Decay Labs recognizable to an international story-first NFT audience, grow a real network on X, convert qualified interest into recurring collection discovery, and earn sales without fake engagement or financial promises.

## Measurement model

Record a baseline before Day 1. Targets are process ranges, not guarantees.

- Leading: primary posts published, meaningful replies, returning commenters, site sessions, lore reads, Subject searches, trust-page visits, Space attendance.
- Quality: unique thoughtful commenters, collector questions, creator conversations, user-generated theories/art, email/Discord retention when live.
- Commercial: verified OpenSea item views, checkout intents, unique buyers and secondary sales.
- Never optimize for: raw follower count alone, bot-like impressions, engagement-pod comments or floor-price screenshots.

## Phase 1 — Foundation and repositioning (Days 1–30)

### Week 1: truth and identity

- Deploy the rebuilt website and verify every public link.
- Update X avatar, banner, bio, location, website and pinned thread.
- Update OpenSea media and description.
- Reduce visible founder inventory toward the curated 24-item policy after wallet review.
- Publish Days 1–7 of the calendar.
- Leave 12–15 human replies daily.
- Output: one coherent identity across X, site, BaseScan references and OpenSea.
- Gate: no broken page, invented number, mismatched contract or inaccessible mobile CTA.

### Week 2: teach the world

- Publish Chapters 001–003 and faction introduction content.
- Run the first Subject Autopsy.
- Publish the trust checklist and project-truth thread.
- Start a private X list of 30 current Base/art/lore accounts.
- Contact three small artists/builders with a concrete shared-artifact idea; no partnership graphic.
- Output: audience can repeat the premise in one sentence.
- Gate: at least five replies demonstrate correct understanding of Vanta/Bloom/witnesses.

### Week 3: participation

- Publish Theory Lab 01 and one ID-roulette interaction.
- Open a waitlist only if a moderated Discord launch date is realistic; otherwise keep discussion on X.
- Host first 30-minute Vanta Radio: “Who owns a memory after its owner is gone?”
- Publish written recap within 24 hours.
- Output: recurring commenters begin talking to each other.
- Gate: two human moderators identified before Discord invite becomes public.

### Week 4: audit and refine

- Complete Days 22–30.
- Compare post formats by meaningful replies, profile visits and site clicks.
- Keep the top two formats; revise the weakest two.
- Publish “what shipped / what failed / what changes next.”
- Prepare Chapter 10 checkpoint rules without opening the vote early.
- Output: first public monthly report.
- Gate: all claims linked to artifacts; no performance cherry-picking.

## Phase 2 — Community and collaboration (Days 31–60)

### Week 5: Discord controlled launch

- Complete the Discord launch gate in `community/discord-setup.md`.
- Invite 20–40 known participants, not an open mass audience.
- Run onboarding manually and collect friction points.
- Start Monday Archive Briefing and Thursday Theory Lab.
- Output: safe, legible server with active staff.
- Gate: scam report and wallet verification tested end-to-end.

### Week 6: first collaborative artifact

- Select one Base-native visual artist or developer based on shipped work.
- Create one artifact: alternate archive transmission, interactive clue, small open-source component or lore illustration.
- Explain roles and compensation/rights transparently.
- Announce only when the artifact is usable or visible.
- Output: collaboration proof, not logo exchange.
- Gate: no vague future cross-promotion promise.

### Week 7: collector depth

- Begin consent-based `Witness Interview` series.
- Spotlight at least one unlisted Subject to keep story independent from sales.
- Review curated listings: remove stale links, avoid undercutting collectors.
- Publish one collector-curated three-Subject exhibition.
- Output: ownership feels like participation, not yield.
- Gate: no holder is pressured to reveal identity or purchase price.

### Week 8: canon checkpoint

- Publish the evidence for Chapter 10 options over several days.
- Run the vote using a transparent, bot-resistant method appropriate to current community size.
- Publish turnout, option definitions, result and permanent decision record.
- Release the story consequence within 72 hours.
- Output: community sees a real bounded choice change canon.
- Gate: outcome is honored even if it is not the founder’s preference.

## Phase 3 — Scale proof, not noise (Days 61–90)

### Week 9: prototype week

- Choose one future direction only: interactive lore viewer, small AI archive interrogation demo or Base-native tool.
- Publish problem statement, working screen, limitations and repository/demo.
- Do not call it utility until users can use it.
- Output: one functioning experiment.
- Gate: no token-gating unless it materially improves the experience.

### Week 10: ecosystem distribution

- Submit the working artifact to relevant Base/Farcaster builder channels.
- Host a technical/artistic Space with the collaborator.
- Create a short case study others can save.
- Ask five credible builders for specific feedback, not reposts.
- Output: external references and qualified visits.
- Gate: attribution and links are correct; no implied endorsement.

### Week 11: narrative release

- Package the next lore sequence as a seven-day event.
- Use memory fragments, two conflicting records and one live theory session.
- Feature community theories with permission.
- Rotate curated inventory only where a Subject directly supports the chapter.
- Output: measurable return behavior across seven days.
- Gate: story remains understandable to a new follower on any entry day.

### Week 12: systemize

- Document moderation, publishing, incident and collaborator workflows.
- Assign human owners for content, lore, community and technical maintenance—even if one person holds several roles.
- Establish a monthly security/link review.
- Prepare the next 30-day calendar from proven formats.
- Output: project can operate without improvising every day.
- Gate: no role depends on shared passwords or seed phrases.

### Days 85–90: public quarter review

- Publish what changed across website, lore, community, collaboration and prototype.
- Report process and quality metrics with dates and sources.
- Name three things that did not work and what will stop.
- Show the next 90-day research direction without guaranteed deliverables.
- Thank contributors specifically and with permission.

## Daily operator checklist

1. Security/link check: five minutes.
2. Read current ecosystem conversations before replying.
3. Write 12–15 contextual replies across three sessions.
4. Publish or prepare one primary artifact.
5. Respond to genuine conversation within reasonable windows.
6. Record metrics and qualitative signal.
7. Document any promise made publicly so it cannot be forgotten.

## Weekly decision rules

- If impressions rise but profile visits and meaningful replies do not, tighten positioning—not posting volume.
- If link clicks rise but Subject views do not, fix landing-page continuity.
- If Subject views rise but marketplace intent does not, improve selection/context before reducing price.
- If sales occur without returning participation, do not call it community growth.
- If a format attracts bots or low-context replies, discontinue it even when headline engagement looks high.
- If operating load exceeds capacity, reduce cadence before quality or safety.
