# 100 high-share post concepts

“Viral” cannot be guaranteed. These ideas are engineered for one clear behavior: reply, repost, save, click or follow. Use one idea at a time and attach genuine evidence or art.

## Lore questions — replies and theories

1. `You wake with someone else’s happiest memory. Do you return it if doing so erases you?`
2. `Three capsules are empty. No door opened. What escaped?`
3. `A warning appears only when nobody is looking. How do you prove it exists?`
4. `Your funeral is dated tomorrow. Who do you call first?`
5. `One city. Five factions. Which theory of decay would you trust with your identity?`
6. `Would you trade one childhood memory to keep your body working?`
7. `Preserve a memory forever—or retain the ability to feel it once more?`
8. `A radio uses a dead scientist’s voice. Do you answer?`
9. `A train can return you to a place erased from history. Where do you go?`
10. `Your mother is alive, younger, and certain you never existed. What evidence could change her mind?`
11. `Subject 1001 appears in every photo but casts no shadow. Give it a name.`
12. `Would you rather know a false past or no past?`
13. `If ownership stabilizes a memory, does transfer count as forgetting?`
14. `Your future self asks you not to become him. Is that warning proof you already did?`
15. `A cure saves your body but removes you from every record. Take it?`
16. `The last verified morning before the apocalypse is for sale. What is your maximum bid—in memories?`
17. `Every purchased memory belongs to somebody else. Is the market theft or rescue?`
18. `The city border loops back from the opposite direction. Are you trapped or infinite?`
19. `One faction must control Vanta for ten chapters. Choose: Rust / Spore / Glass / Static / Bone.`
20. `The moon opens like a door. What is on the other side?`
