# Human reply system

## Daily volume

Start with 12–15 substantive replies per day, six days per week. Raise to 20 only if every reply remains specific. Do not post batches in one minute.

- 5 replies to small/mid-size artists and builders where an actual relationship is possible.
- 4 replies to Base ecosystem posts.
- 3 replies to NFT culture/security/product discussions.
- 3 replies to people who interacted with Decay Labs previously.

## Account selection

Build a private list from current, relevant accounts rather than chasing follower count. Prioritize:

- Base official ecosystem and active Base builders.
- OpenSea product/safety announcements.
- Farcaster/Warpcast builders when the Mini App is relevant.
- Story-first digital art projects and independent artists.
- NFT security researchers and provenance educators.
- Collectors who write thoughtful theses instead of “send alpha.”

Do not predefine a permanent celebrity list: account relevance and quality change. Each Monday, select 30 accounts that posted within the last seven days and whose audience overlaps art, Base, lore or onchain provenance.

## Reply formula

`Specific observation + useful addition or tradeoff + precise question.`

The reply must still be worth reading if the Decay Labs name and avatar are hidden.

## Ready response bank

1. `The distinction between ownership and authorship matters here. The chain can prove who holds the token, but not who gets to define the work’s meaning. How are you handling that boundary?`
2. `This is strongest when the prototype is visible. A 20-second demo would answer more objections than another roadmap graphic—do you have one coming?`
3. `The art direction feels coherent because the material language repeats without cloning the composition. Was the palette fixed before the traits?`
4. `One under-discussed cost of a large supply is narrative attention: every item needs a reason to be remembered. How are you deciding which pieces lead discovery?`
5. `Base reduces transaction friction, but discovery is still a human problem. What channel has brought you returning collectors rather than one-time clicks?`
6. `I would save this as a project trust pattern: state what is live, what is experimental and what is not promised. The last category is often the most credible.`
7. `The security copy is useful. I would add whether verification ever asks for a signature—new collectors often cannot distinguish a message signature from a transaction.`
8. `This is a good example of lore creating behavior instead of decoration. The unresolved question gives people something specific to discuss. Is the answer fixed or community-influenced?`
9. `Curating fewer listings can help discovery, provided it is framed as editorial selection rather than fake scarcity. What story links this release?`
10. `The collector perspective is the missing data point here: what made you return to the project after the first purchase?`
11. `A partnership is believable when one joint artifact exists before the announcement. What is the smallest thing both communities could use next week?`
12. `This trait reads as narrative evidence, not rarity decoration. Does it recur at a specific point in the world’s history?`
13. `Strong composition. The negative space is doing as much brand work as the character. Was this designed for profile cropping too?`
14. `I disagree slightly: lower fees improve access, but they do not create conviction. Conviction still comes from repeated proof and a distinct point of view.`
15. `The best part is that you published the limitation. Knowing what a tool cannot do makes the demo more trustworthy, not less exciting.`

## Replies to avoid

- `Amazing project 🔥`
- `LFG / GM / probably nothing`
- repeating the original post in different words
- dropping the project link without being asked
- tagging unrelated large accounts
- price/floor speculation under art posts
- copy-pasting one reply across accounts

## 20-minute launch window

After each primary Decay Labs post:

1. Answer the first genuine replies quickly.
2. Ask one follow-up question, not “thanks.”
3. Add missing context as a self-reply.
4. Do not delete reasonable criticism; answer with evidence.
5. Move support/security incidents out of public threads only after posting the official ticket path.

## Weekly review

Track replies that produced profile visits, returning commenters, DMs from credible builders, collector questions and invitations. Stop replying to accounts that repeatedly farm engagement without conversation.
