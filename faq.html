Short answers first. Independent links where they matter.

Is there an active mint?
No. All 1,000 tokens already exist. Any available purchase is a secondary listing. There is no whitelist, presale or secret mint.
What does “created on Base” mean?
The collection is an ERC-721 contract on Base mainnet. Ownership and transfers are recorded there and can be checked on BaseScan.
Is the artwork fully onchain?
No such claim is made. Ownership is on Base; artwork and metadata are referenced through IPFS. That distinction is intentional and public.
Who currently controls the supply?
The tokens were initially created into the founder-controlled inventory. Current distribution can change through transfers and must be verified onchain. The project does not use artificial wallets to make holder distribution look larger.
How does in-app collecting work?
The app requests a live OpenSea listing, obtains Seaport fulfillment data and presents the transaction to your connected wallet. Your wallet shows the final transaction before you sign. If no valid listing exists, no purchase should be submitted.
Why does the site avoid rarity rankings?
The current metadata does not contain a defensible rarity system. Factions and memory fragments are narrative classifications. They do not imply price, rarity or future value.
What do collectors receive beyond the token?
They receive the token, its artwork and access to the public narrative system. Canon participation and collector recognition can be offered as community experiences, but no financial return is promised.
Is there staking, a token, an airdrop or a game?
No. None is represented as a delivered feature or guaranteed roadmap item. Small working prototypes will be shown before any future capability is described as real.
Will a moderator DM me?
Official moderators will not DM first with a purchase, mint, whitelist or airdrop link. Never share a seed phrase or private key.
Where are all official links?
Begin at decaylabs.online/links. Verify the contract address before signing anything.
